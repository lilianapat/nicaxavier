== Description ==

Easily include a required checkbox to your site's registration form that ensures your users agree to your terms of use. The checkbox is easily visible, utilizes native integration and styling, and links out to your terms for users to review before signing up. Our included form validation will ensure that if unchecked, the user will receive a notice that they have not agreed to the terms and must do so to complete the process. Simple and effective.
